Case study · Live client

A debt relief site built around one calculation.

DebtFix settles unsecured debt for less than the full balance. The page had to answer one question before anything else: how much is this person paying now, and what would one program deposit be instead. Everything below that supports the answer.

The brief

01 / 04

Somebody with $20,000 of card debt does not read a page. They look for their own number in it. So the whole page is one comparison, with trust proof stacked underneath.

  • One actionA slider, two figures, one button. No form before the payoff.
  • Their numberThe output changes with the slider, so the visitor sees themselves in it.
  • No false promise"Estimates from our plan model, not a quote" sits under the figure.

What I built

02 / 04

  3. The pages around itAbout, how it works, articles, and the contact path, all sharing one header and calculator link.

The calculator

03 / 04

A visitor adds each debt they hold: name, balance, APR, monthly payment. The output is total interest scheduled and months to payoff at the payment they are already making, before they speak to a salesperson. That is the honest framing, and it is also the argument for the product.

InputBehaviour
Balance, APRRequired per debt row. Rows can be added for each card or loan.
Monthly paymentTyped by the visitor, or switched to the issuer minimum.
Minimum payment4% of the current balance or $25, whichever is greater, recalculated every month as the balance falls.
OutputTotal interest and payoff date at the current payment, so the visitor sees the cost of standing still.
