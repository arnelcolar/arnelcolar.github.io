Why We Exist

The opposite of what this industry usually does

Debt relief has an honesty problem. So we built Evenkeel around a different set of defaults:

  • Tell people the truth about what settlement can and cannot do
  • Charge nothing until a debt actually settles
  • Put every settlement decision in the client's hands
  • Stay present through the hard middle months of the program, because that's when it matters
What We Stand For

Four Things We Won't Compromise On

We tell you the truth

If settlement fits your situation, we'll explain exactly how it works. If it doesn't, we'll say so and point you toward what does, even if that means we don't get your business.

We only get paid on results

No upfront fees. Our fee applies only after a debt is actually settled, you approve the settlement, and at least one payment has been made toward it.

You approve every decision

Every settlement offer comes back to you before a dollar moves. Nothing gets negotiated away without your sign-off, ever.

We stay through the hard part

The middle months of a program are where most people need support most. That's when we stay closest, not just at enrollment and not just at the finish line.
