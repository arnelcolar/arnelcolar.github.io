FAQ

Can I upgrade the memory?

Memory is soldered for speed and battery life. Choose 32 GB or 64 GB when you order.

Does it charge over USB-C?

Yes. Either USB-C port accepts power, and the included 140 W charger reaches 50% in 30 minutes.

What comes in the box?

The laptop, a 140 W USB-C charger, a braided cable, and a quick start guide.

What warranty is included?

Two years of parts and labor, with the option to extend to four.
