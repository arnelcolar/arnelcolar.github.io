What people ask most.

What exactly is in the box?

A fanless box the size of a paperback, a 2 TB drive inside, an empty bay for a second drive, a USB-C power supply and a card with a pairing code. No screen, no remote, no cables you have to buy.

Do I need to be technical?

No. You plug it in, scan a code with your phone and pick a password. There is no terminal step, no port forwarding and no account to create.

What happens if the drive dies?

Photos and files are written to two drives. If one fails, the box keeps serving from the other and tells you which one to swap. Drives are standard and replaceable, no tools beyond a screwdriver.

Can I reach it when I am away from home?

Yes, through an encrypted relay that we cannot read, or direct if you prefer to set up your own. Relay access is free and has no bandwidth cap for photos and files.

How much can I store?

2 TB with the included drive, about 650,000 phone photos. The second bay takes drives up to 8 TB, and libraries bigger than that can span several boxes.

Does it really work without the internet?

Yes. Photos, files, music and notes stay available on your home network when the line is down. Only remote access needs the internet.

What does Ask run on?

A small model on the box itself. It reads only the files you point it at, answers with the page it found the answer on, and sends nothing anywhere. No account, no token limits.

Is it a NAS?

It is one, but you do not have to treat it like one. Folders appear in Finder and Explorer, and the apps do the rest.

How do I pay and when am I charged?

You reserve with a card today; nothing is taken until your unit is packed. If you cancel before it ships, you are charged nothing.

Can I cancel?

Any time before your box ships, in one click, for a full refund. After it arrives you have 30 days to send it back.

Which phones and computers work with it?

iPhone and iPad from iOS 16, Android 10 and up, macOS 13 and up, and Windows 10 and 11. The web app works in any modern browser.

Does it work on a mesh network or with a VPN?

Yes. It uses mDNS and a local certificate, so it works behind mesh systems without configuration. If your router isolates clients from each other, turn that setting off.
