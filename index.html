How I work with a customer

The same way every time, inside the tools the team already uses.

  1. 01

    Listen

    Sit with the team and find the question they answer over and over.

  3. 03

    Handover

Before you ask

The five questions that come up first.

It uses AI. Won’t it make things up?
That is the whole job. It answers only from passages in the document, shows the page each answer came from, and refuses what the document does not cover.
Can you work directly with our customers?
Yes. That is the work I want: scope it with them, build it, and be the person they call when something breaks.
What hours do you keep?
UTC+8. That is overnight for the US and same-day for Australia and much of Asia, and I hold four hours of overlap with your team.
Full-time, or contract?
Either. I am looking for a fixed monthly arrangement rather than work priced project by project.
What do you need to start?
The documents, a handful of questions the team answers today, and access to the tool they already use.
